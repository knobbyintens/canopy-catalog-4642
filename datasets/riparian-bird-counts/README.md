# Riparian Bird Counts

**Maintainer:** Avian Survey Unit
**Coverage:** 2019-2026
**Version:** v2.0.1

## Summary

Point-count surveys of riparian bird communities recorded during the spring and autumn migration windows. Counts are aggregated per transect and per survey visit.
